# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Help newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or personal and political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Publicly disclosing a security vulnerability that was reported privately.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and whenever someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, **Hammad Tariq**, at **hammadt451@gmail.com**. Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Depending on severity, the maintainer may:

1. send a private, written warning;
2. temporarily ban the person from interacting with the project;
3. permanently ban them.
